# DigitalTwin-AI-Lab

## Research Laboratory

**DigitalTwin-AI-Lab** is a research laboratory dedicated to the development of intelligent methodologies for monitoring, fault diagnosis, digital twins, artificial intelligence and advanced control of cyber-physical systems.

---

# Vision

To become an internationally recognized research laboratory in Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twins and Intelligent Control applied to industrial and biomedical systems.

---

# Mission

Develop innovative methodologies integrating Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twins and Advanced Control to improve the supervision, diagnosis and operation of cyber-physical systems.

---

# Research Areas

- Digital Twins
- Artificial Intelligence
- Intelligent Monitoring
- Fault Diagnosis
- Advanced Control
- Biomedical Engineering
- Industry 4.0

---

# Current Research Programs

## RP-01 Digital Twin Modeling

Development of digital twins for industrial and biomedical processes.

---

## RP-02 Intelligent Monitoring

AI-based monitoring and anomaly detection.

---

## RP-03 Fault Diagnosis

Detection, isolation and diagnosis of faults using hybrid intelligent models.

---

## RP-04 Intelligent Control

Advanced control, predictive control and adaptive control.

---

## RP-05 Biomedical Systems

Artificial intelligence applied to biomedical signal processing and intelligent medical devices.
